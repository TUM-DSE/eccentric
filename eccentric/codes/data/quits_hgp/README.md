Parity-check matrices of classical (3,4)-regular LDPC codes copied from QUITS
(https://github.com/mkangquantum/quits, `parity_check_matrices/`, MIT license, see `LICENSE`).
`n` is the block length and `dist` the classical distance; the `arXiv2311_03307` matrices are from arXiv:2311.03307.
The hypergraph product of a matrix with itself gives the `hgp225`, `hgp625` and `hgp900` codes.
