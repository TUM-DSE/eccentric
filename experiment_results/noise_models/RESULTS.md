## Key observations
1. **Readout-length U-shape, optimum ~600 ns** (models 1-2): 200 ns saturates (flip error 0.221); 600 ns is the sweet spot; 1000 ns slightly worse (decoherence).
2. **Readout flip dominates over gate errors:** Model 1 ~ Model 2 (zeroing SX/CZ barely moves LER); Model 3 (no readout flip) is far lower.
3. **Decoherence-only (Model 3) threshold behaviour:** Bacon-Shor is solidly **sub-threshold (LER ~ 0 at all d, incl. d=13)**. Surface/color/hh are sub-threshold only at low d (d=5,7) and go **over threshold by d=9-11** (LER rises with d to ~0.5). With reset now noiseless (R=0) the low-d decoherence dropped vs the earlier R=1.294us run.
4. **Gross saturates at LER ~ 1.0** in all models (12 logical qubits, far over threshold).
5. **Models 1 & 2 are over threshold for d >= 9-11** across surface/color/hh even at the 600 ns optimum (LER ~ 0.5), driven by the HERQULES readout flip + decoherence.
